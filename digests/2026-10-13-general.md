## AI

1. **Meta launches Muse Wonder and Muse Wonder Full**: frontier-class models for agents, memory, coding and computer use. Muse Wonder costs $4/$20 per million input/output tokens via the Meta Model API and Muse Code; Full is only on Muse Code subscriptions. Oct 12.
   - <https://www.axios.com/2026/10/12/meta-new-ai-models-anthropic-openai>
   - <https://dev.meta.ai/resources/blog/build-with-muse-wonder>
2. **Character.AI files for Chapter 11 bankruptcy**: court-supervised sale after years of lawsuits; Disney is lead bidder at $20M for a non-exclusive IP license, and the platform shuts down if no buyer keeps it running. Filed Oct 12.
   - <https://www.theverge.com/ai-artificial-intelligence/1009802/character-ai-files-for-bankruptcy-disney>
   - <https://techcrunch.com/2026/10/13/just-5-years-in-interactive-chatbot-platform-character-ai-files-for-bankruptcy/>
   - <https://blog.character.ai/an-update-on-character-ai/>
3. **Google DeepMind's AlphaProof Nexus solves open math problems**: reportedly 9 open Erdős problems and 44 OEIS conjectures, Lean-verified, published in Science. Oct 12. Secondary source only; confirm against DeepMind or Science.
   - <https://aitoolherald.com/articles/google-deepmind-s-alphaproof-nexus-solves-open-math-problems/>
4. **UC San Diego trains a 7B agent with "theory of mind"**: NeurIPS 2026 paper (arXiv:2610.12453); ~11.5% relative gains on Sotopia, +26.7 points on BigToM. Oct 12.
   - <https://cryptobriefing.com/uc-san-diego-theory-of-mind-7b-agent/>
5. **HN: GLM-Legal (z.ai)**: legal-domain model, 608 points, 336 comments. <https://news.ycombinator.com/>
6. **HN: "AI models can provably hide arbitrary capabilities"**: arXiv paper, 237 points. Also: the math behind OpenAI's faster Fourier transform claim (argmin.net) and "AI is a tool and using it well takes skill" (474 points). <https://news.ycombinator.com/>
