**Biggest story:** Google shipped [Gemini 4 Argon](https://x.com/sundarpichai/status/2110038898009407584) globally — Sundar’s post hit ~22.5K likes and ~3.8M views.

## Launches

- [Gemini 4 Argon](https://x.com/sundarpichai/status/2110038898009407584), ~22.5K likes / 3.8M views. Google’s new frontier model is live for API, Antigravity (Ultra), and Gemini app/Search (Pro + Ultra), with intro pricing at $2/$10 per million tokens.
- [Cursor adds Argon](https://x.com/cursor_ai/status/2110087611763368271), ~3.2K likes. Available in Cursor at 52.8% on CursorBench (default effort), on par with Opus 5.5.
- [Physical Intelligence Pi API beta](https://x.com/physical_int/status/2110115571681690051), ~1.3K likes / 171K views. Teams can upload robot data, fine-tune π models on managed compute, and run hosted inference (strawberry picking to server assembly already in the wild).
- [Liquid AI LFM2.5-24B-A2B](https://x.com/liquidai/status/2110055807194931348), ~2.5K likes / 201K views. Open-weight MoE (~24B total, ~2B active) with 131K context for local agent work on laptops and PCs.
