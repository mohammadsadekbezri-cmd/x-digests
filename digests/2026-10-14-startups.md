**Biggest story:** [Ramp reportedly raised $1.85B at a $60B valuation](https://x.com/TradedVC/status/2110089114544480696), led by Dragoneer and Thrive Capital with Founders Fund, up from $44B just four months ago.

## New startups and funding

- [Ramp, $1.85B at $60B](https://x.com/TradedVC/status/2110089114544480696). The corporate-card company says it's pushing deeper into AI agents that handle finance work; Bloomberg first reported the round.
- [TypeSafe AI, $870M Series A at $7.5B](https://x.com/Investclubsv/status/2109932187696898063). Led by a16z with Sequoia and DCVC, just weeks after launching Jev, a model that returns decisions with probabilities instead of text.
- [Applied Intuition fielding offers at $30B+](https://x.com/theinformation/status/2110090717838114933). The Information says the self-driving software maker's value would double from $15B in June as it moves into autonomous military vehicles.
- [Field AI, reportedly $700M at $10B](https://x.com/Innov8_Energy/status/2110015328008712481). The physical-AI robotics startup more than quadrupled its $2B valuation in about a year.
- [Oratomic, $475M Series B at $5.4B](https://x.com/DogQiao/status/2109994487954936237). A quantum computing round reported by the WSJ.
- [Atlas Atomics, $400M Series A at $1.9B](https://x.com/shortsqueeznews/status/2110009135429488971). The nuclear startup co-founded by Vivek Ramaswamy, reportedly led by General Catalyst with a16z.
- [OpenEvidence, $250M at $15B](https://x.com/25_ventures/status/2110189240462004564). The AI clinical-search tool for doctors is now also working on cancer drugs, per posts.
- [ICON, $200M Series D](https://x.com/dealroomnews/status/2110019619104690469). The 3D-printed homes company's round was co-led by BlackRock and Baillie Gifford, taking total funding to $700M.
- [Biossil, $153M at $1B](https://x.com/ForgiveMySyn/status/2110068475766915406). A Toronto startup using AI to revive shelved drug candidates, in a round led by OpenAI.
- [Fasset becomes a UAE unicorn](https://x.com/foundingmena/status/2110234146878689552). The interest-free digital bank raised $68M at a $1B valuation, three months after a $51M round.
- [Hone, $60M seed at $285M](https://x.com/SharClayton/status/2109959223371247808). An AI agent startup backed by Benchmark and Index Ventures.
- [Albert, $55M Series B at $340M](https://x.com/techsnif/status/2110166716579733868). It bills itself as a chemistry-native AI for materials R&D.
- [Pluto Security, $42M out of stealth](https://x.com/TechCrunch/status/2110057752747708773). It monitors and controls what AI agents and employee-built AI apps do inside companies; Jump Capital, TLV Partners, and Ansa Capital invested.
- [Causa Prima, $20M seed](https://x.com/TFNBreakingNews/status/2109997021801468316). An agent-to-agent platform for billing disputes, led by Base10 with QED and FJ Labs.
- [Replicas, $4M seed](https://x.com/connortbot/status/2110037854386843677), ~450 likes. A "harness-agnostic" cloud coding agent backed by YC, Flex Capital, and Paul Graham.
- [Interfere, $5.1M seed](https://x.com/lukeshiels/status/2110055610423431200), ~264 likes. Led by Hummingbird with YC and Vercel, building tools to help software teams ship polished products.
- [Bloomy (YC S26), $3M seed](https://x.com/bloomylearning/status/2110079947385749869). A K–12 learning platform backed by YC, Reach Capital, and Robinhood.
- [Sarvam in talks for $300M at $3B](https://x.com/cvkrishnan/status/2109984964012122307), ~309 likes. India's AI lab wants the money for bigger models and compute.

## Product launches

- [Fish Audio Drama 3](https://x.com/FishAudio/status/2110038429199450126), ~11.9K likes / 8.1M views. An open-weight text-to-speech model where you can direct the emotion of single words in plain English; the company also cited a $52M seed and 15M+ users.
- [Cursor Origin app marketplace](https://x.com/cursor_ai/status/2110075624253751796), ~100 likes. Twenty-plus integrations for CI, deploys, code review, and security (Vercel, Cloudflare, Linear, CodeRabbit), now open to public submissions.
- [MagicOS](https://x.com/alexdanilowicz/status/2110030443466940671), ~653 likes / 266K views. A design tool betting that designers keep the canvas and AI works inside it, instead of agents running off on their own.
- [Sazabi 1.0](https://x.com/shcallaway/status/2110038490859855990), ~419 likes / 218K views. Built for teams whose agents merge their own PRs and deploy hundreds of times a day.
- [RouterPlus (YC F26)](https://x.com/alokbishoyi97/status/2110022808092967160), ~373 likes / 189K views. It picks the best model and provider for each workload at the lowest price, using one key.
- [Glass](https://x.com/geoffintech/status/2110015920907194553), ~373 likes / 490 bookmarks. A platform for rolling out AI across a company so the gains compound instead of stopping at "everyone has a chatbot."
- [useABI](https://x.com/thediary_keeper/status/2110018355046338988), ~442 likes. An AI associate for M&A teams, built by a former Greenberg Traurig deal lawyer.
- [Artificial Societies' Radiant](https://x.com/societies_ai/status/2110011079757529441), ~163 likes. A simulation platform for testing how people and groups will react before big decisions.
- [Facebook "Your Algorithm"](https://x.com/TechCrunch/status/2110070333864558828). Meta now lets Facebook users steer their feed's AI-powered ranking.
- [Google's personal Android apps](https://x.com/TechCrunch/status/2110055233044824251), ~103 likes. You can now build AI-made Android apps for yourself without publishing them on the Play Store.
- [Opendoor Own](https://x.com/GMN_watch/status/2110084357423849861), ~290 likes. A new Opendoor home product, launching only in North Carolina for now.
- [Razer Sylveon headset](https://x.com/Dexerto/status/2110098320324497541), ~40K likes. A $110 wired RGB headset for Pokémon's 30th anniversary, the most-liked consumer launch of the day.

## Founder takes

- [Paul Graham: ask what customers would buy](https://x.com/paulg/status/2110086189386695130), ~3.6K likes. If they won't buy your product, ask what they would buy; few founders do.
- [Paul Graham: the old advice still holds](https://x.com/paulg/status/2110087363254690301), ~834 likes. Nearly all standard startup advice still applies in the AI era, at least for now.
- [Travis Kalanick on the founder's paradox](https://x.com/a16z/status/2110084478731510212), ~897 likes. The better you get at building companies, the harder it is to stay fired up.
- [Lenny Rachitsky on real moats](https://x.com/lennysan/status/2110059109685113206), ~677 likes / 1.1K bookmarks. Most of what people call moats, distribution included, doesn't create lasting defensibility.
- [Railway's lean growth](https://x.com/nakul/status/2110053419067322566), ~575 likes. It went from $20M to about $100M ARR this year with a solo founder and about 40 people.
- [Raising a $30M seed in 7 days](https://x.com/kozerafilip/status/2110008979980185954), ~480 likes / 1K bookmarks. Filip Kozera's two-minute guide to running a first VC round.
